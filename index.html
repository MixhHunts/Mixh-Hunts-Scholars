Apply to the Mixh Hunts Scholars Programme

Welcome. Complete a short five-step form, upload your documents, review everything, and submit. It takes about 15 minutes, and you will get a reference number to track your application.

How it works

  1. Personal details
    Who you are and how to reach you.
  2. Education
    Your qualification and institution.
  3. Additional information
    Category, motivation, emergency contact.
  4. Documents
    Upload photo, ID and certificates.
  5. Review and submit
    Check, edit and send.

Application requirements

🎓

Qualification

At least a secondary school certificate or equivalent. Graduates and final-year students may apply.

🪪

Valid identification

National ID, international passport, driver’s licence or voter’s card (PDF, JPG or PNG, max 2 MB).

📷

Passport photograph

Recent, plain background, JPG or PNG, max 2 MB.

📞

Working contacts

An active phone number, email address and one emergency contact.

Frequently asked questions

Can I save my application and finish later?

This prototype keeps your progress only while the page is open. A connected backend can save drafts to your account.

Is there an application fee?

No fee is set in this prototype. Your organization can add a payment step later.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, JPG and PNG, up to 2 MB per file. The passport photograph must be JPG or PNG.

How do I check my application status?

Use “Track application” in the menu and enter your reference number.

Can I edit my application after submitting?

You can edit everything on the review step before you submit. After submission, contact the admissions office.
